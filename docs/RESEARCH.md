# Research and design decisions

> Follow-up: the original site became accessible again. Its non-blog pages, map records, media, and donation inventory have now been recovered. The user explicitly excluded transferring the old blog articles. The sections below document the initial design/research stage; see [MIGRATION.md](MIGRATION.md) for current scope and implementation status.

Research conducted September 18, 2026. Implementation follows the user-approved direction to redesign and develop abundantearthfoundation.org.

## Positioning

AEF’s distinctive role is connecting locally led projects with grants, relationships, teaching, and practical support. The redesign leads with community agency and clear outcomes, then introduces permaculture and regenerative agriculture in accessible language.

The visual concept uses a restrained forest-green, cream, and ochre palette, Fraunces headlines, DM Sans body copy, authentic project photography, and limited animation. The botanical mark is a redesign concept, not an assertion that it is the old official logo.

## Audiences and journeys

1. **Supporter:** homepage → project or impact → giving → verified provider, or giving inquiry when unavailable.
2. **Project leader:** get support → current availability message → short email inquiry; fiscal sponsorship explained separately.
3. **Volunteer/partner:** get involved → relevant opportunity page → email inquiry.
4. **Returning reader:** stories/resources → project profile → newsletter provider or subscription inquiry.

## Findings that shaped implementation

- The archived homepage led with “Join the Permaculture Movement,” a phrase clearer to existing practitioners than to first-time donors. The redesign starts with the foundation’s practical role.
- Long project descriptions and historical lists were interleaved. The redesign separates the directory, individual profiles, stories, and reporting context.
- The archived site reported 150+ projects in over 30 countries, while older sections used smaller totals. The redesign treats figures as historical and centrally maintains the headline metrics.
- ARC’s large cumulative results represent its partner network. These are not claimed as outcomes caused solely by AEF funding.
- The January 2022 funding page combined an “applications closed” notice, a return date without a year, and a lengthy form. The redesign asks visitors to confirm current availability and offers a short first inquiry.
- The May 2025 financials archive linked filings through 2021. ProPublica lists newer filings through fiscal 2024, included with source links.
- ARC still linked donors to AEF’s legacy donation URLs. Redirects preserve those entries and project context.
- Beyond Organic Design’s current domain redirects to Eco-Designers. Its live website still links to `/bod`; the new route preserves that inbound path. The historical AEF relationship is kept distinct from current partner material.
- Facebook and Instagram did not expose complete feeds. No social engagement audit, current follower metrics, or content-performance claims are inferred from incomplete search results.

## Scope and remaining content recovery

The launch code includes six curated project profiles and three editorial archive stories. It is not an exhaustive recovery of all 150+ projects, original blog posts, award recipients, or uploaded documents. Recover the original WordPress export/database/uploads if available before claiming a complete archive migration.

The existing research identifies additional initiatives such as SunRise Studios, Rooted Connections, Art of Love Letters, ReGenerOsa, Ancient Cacao, and the Charles Perry Eckberg Memorial Fund. These require fuller source review and current owner confirmation before dedicated fundraising pages are added. Unknown old URLs correctly return a helpful 404; they are not all redirected to unrelated content.

The directory uses accessible country links and combined filters. A geographic visualization can be added later using confirmed project coordinates; no precise project locations have been invented.
